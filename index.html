Human review for AI agents: paid per task

At blackcode, AI agents do most of the work. Some steps still need a person: a careful look on a real phone, a check of a short video, a second opinion on two texts, a first-time-user test of an app. We pay people who already work with AI to do these steps, per task, like gig work for software.

The work

  • Short tasks you do on your own schedule, before a deadline.
  • Every task has a clear goal, the material you need, and a short answer form: what you saw, screenshots, how sure you are.
  • Examples: "Open this test page on your phone and report anything broken", "Watch this 2-minute video and list every mistake", "Which of these two texts is clearer, and why?"
  • You only look and report. You never act for us, never contact anyone for us, and never handle money or logins.

How joining works

  1. Apply with the 5-minute form below. Instead of a CV, you can send a skills summary made from your own AI memory (see the next section). Prefer not to? There's a plain path too.
  2. A short video call if we shortlist you.
  3. Three paid trial tasks at the full rate.
  4. Then regular tasks, as many or as few as you like. This first round is a 3-week pilot with 2–3 people.

Your AI memory is the new CV (optional)

If you work with AI every day, your AI's memory (ChatGPT or Claude memory, Obsidian notes, an MCP memory server) shows what you really do better than a CV. So we ask for a summary, not the memory itself:

  1. Paste the prompt below into your own AI assistant, the one that knows your work.
  2. It writes a short skills summary, with no names, companies, clients or personal details.
  3. You read it, remove anything you don't want to share, and paste it into the form.

It runs on your side. We never see your conversations or your memory. It's fully optional: the plain path (a few questions about your work) counts the same.

Using what you know about my work from our past conversations and your memory, write a skills summary for a job application, in English, max 250 words.
Rules: do NOT include any names of people, companies, clients, products, places, emails, phone numbers, links, or anything private about me or others. Describe kinds of work, not specific projects.
Sections:
1. What kinds of work I do with AI (3-5 bullets)
2. Tools and AI agents I use regularly, and for what
3. Two examples of problems I solved, described generically
4. What I'm careful about or notice that others miss
5. Roughly how long I have been working with AI tools
End with: "Generated by my AI assistant; reviewed and edited by me."

  • We never ask you to pay anything, share a password, or install anything to apply. Anyone saying otherwise "from blackcode" is not us.
